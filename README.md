# Coding-ideas-for-ai

Artificial intelligence ideas and such.

## Project concept: "Skyline Siblings"

A simple 2D platformer where the player controls a clever mechanic, Jun, and their
AI companion drone, Pico. The world is a floating city recovering from a tech
collapse. The goal is to restore critical systems and help communities reconnect
— not rescue a damsel.

### Core pillars

- **Platforming + puzzle flow:** short stages with movement challenges, switches,
  and environmental hazards.
- **AI companion gameplay:** Pico predicts moving platforms, highlights safe
  paths, and learns from the player's route to adjust hints.
- **Community-forward narrative:** missions center on repairing infrastructure
  and empowering locals.

### Bechdel test compliance

- Jun regularly speaks with other women (e.g., Chief Engineer Nila, Archivist
  Rana) about fixing the grid, rerouting power, and planning upgrades.
- Dialogue focuses on the city, tools, and strategy, not romantic interests.

### High-level loop

1. **Scout:** Pico maps a route by analyzing hazards.
2. **Traverse:** Jun uses wall-jumps, grapples, and timed boosts.
3. **Restore:** cooperative puzzle to bring a node online.
4. **Debrief:** local residents share new upgrade plans.

### Sample level theme

"Windworks District": rotating turbines and gust mechanics. Jun and Nila plan
how to reroute power to keep the windmills stable while Pico tracks safe
intervals.
